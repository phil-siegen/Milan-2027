Venue & Travel

Università degli Studi di Milano

Both events take place at the University of Milan. Building and room will be announced here.

Getting there

Travel

By plane. Milan is served by Linate (closest to the city, metro line M4 to the centre), Malpensa (Malpensa Express train to Cadorna or Centrale, about 50 minutes) and Bergamo Orio al Serio (shuttle buses to Centrale, about an hour).

By train. Milano Centrale has direct high-speed connections to Turin, Bologna, Florence, Rome and Zurich, and EuroCity trains to Switzerland and Germany.

In the city. The venue is reachable by metro and tram; a single ATM ticket covers 90 minutes of travel.

Staying

Accommodation

Travel and accommodation of invited speakers and teachers are covered by the University of Siegen; we will book rooms for you and be in touch about the details.

Summer school participants arrange their own accommodation. A list of suggested hotels and hostels near the venue will follow.
