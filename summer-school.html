Programme

What the school covers

Each day is dedicated to one overarching topic. It consists of two 90-minute lecture-style sessions in the morning and around midday, which leave room for discussion, and one 90-minute seminar in the afternoon in which the group works through key texts together. Each session is led by a different teacher. Participants are expected to prepare the readings in advance; the literature list will be published here.

The detailed thematic outline will be published together with the list of teachers.

Application

How to apply

Please send a short CV and a letter of motivation (max. one page) stating your background and your interest in the topic to cord.friebe@uni-siegen.de. The application deadline will be announced here.

Participants of the summer school are warmly invited to stay on for the workshop on 9th–10th September.

Scholarships for students of the University of Siegen. A limited number of scholarships of €500 each is available for summer school participants from the Universität Siegen. Please indicate in your application if you would like to be considered. We look forward to your applications!
