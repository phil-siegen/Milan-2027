Workshop · 9th–10th September 2027

Ongoing Processes and Biological Individuality

Two days of talks on whether and how a process ontology can account for the individuation, persistence and boundaries of living systems. We expect both detailed investigations within process metaphysics and critical positions towards it, and therefore look forward to a particularly extensive discussion.

Theme

Questions we want to discuss

What makes an ongoing process one process, and what makes it the process of one organism? Can the persistence conditions of living beings be stated without reference to substances? How do symbiosis, metamorphosis and reproduction look from a process perspective, and what does that imply for the debate on biological individuality?
